Duplex Specifications & Stay Guidelines

Duplex Premium Features

  • Total area of 176m²+ (Spacious living spaces and panoramic open terraces).
  • 2 Large bedrooms (4x4.5m and 5x4.5m) dressed with premium linens.
  • Grand traditional Moroccan salon (4.5m x 5.4m) for authentic relaxation.
  • Fully equipped modern kitchen with all necessary utensils and appliances.
  • 2 Massive private terraces showcasing unobstructed Atlantic sunset views.
  • Maximum occupancy: 5 guests (Up to 2 extra children under 7 allowed upon prior notice).
  • Complimentary High-Speed WiFi.
  • 1 Dedicated secure vehicle parking spot inside the private premises.

Arrival Tips & Policies

  • Exclusively curated for families, couples, and respectful traveler groups.
  • Standard local regulations apply: Presentation of marriage certificate for Moroccan couples.
  • Check-in procedure: A quick local registration form is required upon arrival.
  • Strictly Non-Smoking inside the apartment. Allowed on terraces only.
  • Safety Warning: Exercise caution when swimming during periods of strong Atlantic cross-currents.
Essential Provisioning Notice:

Local village shops close early in the evening. We highly recommend bringing your major water bottles and fresh groceries before arrival.
