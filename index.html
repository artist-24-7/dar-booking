Duplex Specifications & Stay Guidelines

Discover the perfect harmony of authentic Moroccan heritage and modern coastal living tailored for global travelers.

Duplex Premium Features

  • Total area of 176m²+ featuring a rare, high-value mixed architectural style that seamlessly balances modern layouts with traditional aesthetics.
  • 2 Massive private terraces offering a 100% direct, completely unobstructed Atlantic view with absolutely no front-row houses or restrictions blocking your sunset.
  • Classy, elegant bedrooms furnished with comfortable modern beds for peaceful nights.
  • Grand upper-floor traditional Moroccan salon featuring premium authentic woodwork (wood craftsmanship) and a dedicated dining table space.
  • Cozy, fully-equipped modern kitchen and integrated dining area—designed to be highly practical, warm, and comforting without unnecessary, clinical fancy over-styling.
  • Surrounded by local seaside agriculture fields, allowing an immersive eco-tourism experience to watch local farming communities at work.
  • Maximum occupancy: 5 guests (Up to 2 extra children under 7 allowed upon prior notice).
  • Complimentary High-Speed WiFi & secure private vehicle parking on premises.

Arrival Tips & Policies

  • Exclusively curated for families, couples, and respectful travelers from Europe, Asia, and worldwide looking for premium tranquility.
  • Standard regulations apply: Presentation of marriage certificate for Moroccan couples.
  • Check-in procedure: A quick local registration form is required upon arrival.
  • Strictly Non-Smoking inside the apartment. Allowed on ocean-facing terraces only.
  • Safety Warning: Exercise caution when swimming during periods of strong Atlantic cross-currents.
Essential Provisioning Notice:

Local village shops close early in the evening. We highly recommend bringing your major water bottles and fresh groceries before arrival.
